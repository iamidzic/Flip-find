Privacy Policy — Flip & Find

Last updated: July 6, 2026

Flip & Find (“the app”) is developed by Amikos. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. The short version: we don’t want your data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking.

Data We Do Not Collect

Flip & Find does not collect, store, or share:

  • Your name, email address, or any account information (the app has no sign-up)
  • Your location
  • Your contacts, photos, camera, or microphone
  • Analytics or usage statistics
  • Advertising identifiers (the app contains no ads and no tracking SDKs)

Data Stored on Your Device

Your game progress — completed levels, best times, unlocked card packs, and settings — is stored locally on your device. It never leaves your device and is deleted if you delete the app.

In-App Purchases (RevenueCat)

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. To validate and restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. RevenueCat receives:

  • An anonymous, randomly generated app user ID (not linked to your identity)
  • Your purchase history for this app (which packs you bought)
  • Basic device information (device model, OS version, app version)

This data is used solely to deliver and restore your purchases. RevenueCat does not sell this data. You can read RevenueCat’s privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy. We never see or store your payment details — those are handled entirely by Apple.

Game Center (Optional)

If you choose to sign in to Game Center, your scores are submitted to Apple’s Game Center leaderboards under your Game Center player identity. This is managed entirely by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy. If you don’t use Game Center, no scores are shared.

Support Emails

If you contact us through the in-app “Contact Support” option, your email will be pre-filled with the app version, your device’s OS version, and your anonymous purchase ID. This helps us troubleshoot purchase issues. You can delete any of this before sending. We use the information you send only to respond to your request.

Children’s Privacy

Flip & Find is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal information, no personal information is collected from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will update it here and revise the “Last updated” date above. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at info@amikos.io.
